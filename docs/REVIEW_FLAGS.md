# NammaNudi Content Review Flags (v3)

All items below carry `reviewed: false` and are flagged for native Kannada speaker verification with explicit rationales.

---

## 1. Words & Particles Verification Flags

1. **`word-thumba` (ತುಂಬಾ / `thumba`)**:
   - Spelled `thumba` (not `thumpa`) across all data. Reviewer should verify whether the aspirate `th` is preferred in Roman transliteration or plain `tumba`.
2. **`word-swalpa` (ಸ್ವಲ್ಪ / `swalpa`)**:
   - Spoken usage extends from "a little" to an omnibus politeness marker ("excuse me / please / gently"). Reviewer to confirm if English explanation "a little / please" captures this nuance clearly for beginners.
3. **`word-gottu` (ಗೊತ್ತು / `gottu`) & `word-baruththe` (ಬರುತ್ತೆ / `baruththe`)**:
   - Dative syntax flagged: Beginners frequently make the mistake of saying `"Naanu gottu"` or `"Naanu baruththe"`. Reviewer to check if the tip emphasizing `"Nanage gottu"` and `"Nanage baruththe"` is sufficient.
4. **`word-chennaagidhe` (ಚೆನ್ನಾಗಿದೆ / `chennaagidhe`)**:
   - Animate vs Inanimate distinction: `chennaagidhe` is for things/food, while `chennaagiddhira?` is for people. Flagged to ensure learners don't say `chennaagidheya` to humans.
5. **`word-kodi` (ಕೊಡಿ / `kodi`) vs `kodu` (ಕೊಡು / `kodu`)**:
   - Politeness tip flagged: Emphasizes that `kodu` should never be used with elders, service workers, or auto drivers in Karnataka. Reviewer to confirm tone.

## 2. Numbers, Money & Time Regional Flags (`numbers-time`)

1. **`num-02` (ಚಿಲ್ಲರೆ / `chillare` — Change)**:
   - In Bengaluru and Mysore, `chillare` is standard. In coastal and northern Karnataka, words like `khullaa` (ಖುಲ್ಲಾ) or `change` are also common.
2. **`num-04` (ಹತ್ತೂವರೆ / `haththoo-vare` — 10:30) & `num-05` (ಕಾಲು ಗಂಟೆ / `kaalu gante` — 15 mins)**:
   - Fractional time markers (`kaalu` = 1/4, `ardha` = 1/2, `mukkaalu` = 3/4, `-vare` = and a half). Reviewer should check if `haththoo-vare` pronunciation is natural for non-native speakers.
3. **`num-08` (ಬ್ಯಾಲೆನ್ಸ್ / `balance` vs ಚಿಲ್ಲರೆ / `chillare`)**:
   - Cash transactions in Bengaluru shops frequently use the English loanword `balance` (`balance kodi`). Flagged for reviewer confirmation vs `baaki hana` (ಬಾಕಿ ಹಣ - more formal).
4. **`num-09` (ತಾರೀಖು / `thaareekhu` vs ದಿನಾಂಕ / `dhinaanka`)**:
   - Colloquial spoken Kannada widely uses `thaareekhu` (ತಾರೀಖು) for calendar dates; `dhinaanka` (ದಿನಾಂಕ) is literary/formal.

## 3. Directions & Landmarks Flags (`directions`)

1. **`dir-01` & `dir-02` (`ರೈಟ್` / `ಲೆಫ್ಟ್` vs `ಬಲಗಡೆ` / `ಎಡಗಡೆ`)**:
   - In spoken Bengaluru Kannada, auto drivers and pedestrians almost universally say `right thagolli` and `left thagolli` rather than `balagade` (ಬಲಗಡೆ) and `edagade` (ಎಡಗಡೆ). Reviewer to verify keeping English loanwords for spoken realism.
2. **`dir-04` (`ಫ್ಲೈಓವರ್ ಮೇಲೆ ಹೋಗ್ಬೇಕಾ, ಕೆಳಗೆ ಹೋಗ್ಬೇಕಾ?`)**:
   - Crucial Bengaluru traffic phrase (e.g. Silk Board, Hebbal, Electronic City flyovers). Reviewer to check naturalness.
3. **`dir-05` (`ಡಿವೈಡರ್ ಕಟ್` / `divider cut` & `ಯೂ-ಟರ್ನ್` / `U-turn`)**:
   - Colloquial street vocabulary used by commuters to guide drivers.

## 4. Home & Neighbours Flags (`home-neighbours`)

1. **`home-01` (`ಪಾರ್ಸೆಲ್ ಸೆಕ್ಯುರಿಟಿ ಹತ್ರ ಇಟ್ಟು ಹೋಗಿ`)**:
   - Everyday apartment delivery instruction. Reviewer should verify whether `ittu hogi` (ಇಟ್ಟು ಹೋಗಿ) or `kottu hogi` (ಕೊಟ್ಟು ಹೋಗಿ) is more natural.
2. **`home-02` (`ಕಸ ಗಾಡಿ` / `kasa gaadi` — Garbage truck)**:
   - BBMP waste collection van phrase. Reviewer to confirm timing and natural phrasing.
3. **`home-03` (`ಕರೆಂಟ್ ಹೋಗಿದೆ` / `current hogidhe` — Power cut)**:
   - Colloquial expression for power cut (BESCOM outage) using `current hogidhe`.
4. **`home-07` (`ದಯವಿಟ್ಟು ಸ್ವಲ್ಪ ಸೌಂಡ್ ಕಮ್ಮಿ ಮಾಡಿ`)**:
   - Polite apartment neighbor request. Reviewer to verify if `sound kammi maadi` strikes the right balance between politeness and firmness.

## 5. Greetings & Social Flags (`greetings-social`)

1. **`greet-03` (`ಊಟ ಆಯ್ತಾ?` / `Oota aaythaa?`)**:
   - Culturally foundational Kannada greeting (equivalent to "How are you? / Hope all is well"). Reviewer to check pedagogical explanation for non-Kannadigas who might take it literally as an invitation to eat.
2. **`greet-09` (`ಮತ್ತೆ ಸಿಗೋಣ, ಬರ್ತೀನಿ!` / `Mathe sigona, bartheeni!`)**:
   - Traditional Kannada etiquette dictates that one never says "I will go" (`hogtheeni`), but rather "I will come/take leave" (`bartheeni`). Reviewer to confirm tip.
